## ProxyPass is built in

* Sign in with your **Microsoft/Xbox account** through device code, then use the selected online Bedrock server through `127.0.0.1:19132`.
* Added the built-in ProxyPass relay for current Bedrock protocol `2168` / Minecraft `1.26.42`.
* Your Xbox gamertag and avatar now update the OrionBE profile after sign-in.

## Diagnostics when you need them

* ProxyPass has an optional in-app terminal with a **Copy logs** button for connection and authentication reports.
* Leave the terminal disabled during normal play to avoid buffering UI logs.

## Packaging

* Linux AppImage release artifacts are self-contained, single-file builds and now use the release version in their filename.
